## Where does the manual work actually hide?

{/* INSTRUCTION: open with the answer, then include one cited statistic. */}
It hides in the work nobody schedules: the daily budget check, the pacing spreadsheet, the manual note that a campaign drifted. According to ppc.land, about 87% of agencies pace budgets by hand, and 71% of ad-ops teams say that manual work puts campaigns at risk.

**The work that never gets scheduled is the work that never gets measured, and it is the first place margin leaks.**

## What does it cost when you add it up?

{/* INSTRUCTION: convert the hours into P&L language, never "time saved". */}
Framed as capacity rather than hours, a senior strategist can lose the better part of a week each month to tasks a system could hold. That is billable capacity spent on maintenance instead of on winning the next account.

**Every hour of manual pacing is an hour of senior capacity you are paying for twice.**

## Common questions

### Is manual ad ops really that expensive?

The expense is hidden because it never lands on an invoice, but it shows up as the extra strategist you hire to keep pace. That is the cost.

### Does automation replace the strategist?

No. It removes the maintenance so the strategist spends their hours on the judgement calls that actually move accounts.
